## Optional Veo prompt: paper held against light

Create an 8-second, 16:9 macro film of handmade off-white paper on a dark workbench. Begin with soft front lighting so only the paper fibers are visible. From second 2, a warm light moves behind the sheet. A subtle, irregular geometric wire-mould watermark becomes visible as a difference in paper thickness. Hold on the texture for the final 2 seconds. One continuous camera move, shallow depth of field, restrained museum-documentary look. No text, logos, interface, futuristic effects, or glowing circuitry. The watermark should look physically part of the paper, not printed ink. Silent.

The supplied GIF now fills this role on slide 10. For a future replacement, use this as a conceptual transition. Keep any production context in presenter notes. Keep the Library of Congress photograph as the historical example.
